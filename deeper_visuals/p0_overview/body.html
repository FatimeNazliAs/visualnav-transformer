P0 / Overview · static primer

One trained model, two ways to drive

A driving brain for a wheeled robot: camera pictures in, steering commands out.

What it does

  • Drives a wheeled robot from camera images alone — no map, no laser scanner, no GPS.
  • Watches the last four camera frames, and optionally one photo of the destination.
  • Answers with the next eight driving commands, recomputed several times a second.
  • Trained once, on recordings of robots driven around real buildings. One weight file, never swapped at run time.

In and out

goes in

Pictures

  • 4 recent camera frames, t−3 … t
  • 1 goal photo, t + 8 — optional
  • colour, 96 × 96 pixels each

comes out

Driving commands

  • 8 steps of (speed, turn)
  • an estimate of how far the goal is
  • redrawn every control cycle

Core architecture

  • 2picture readers
  • 5tokens compared
  • 256numbers per token
  • 10cleanup passes

What it can do

goal token kept

Navigation

  • Give it a photo of the destination.
  • It drives toward that place, steering around what is in the way.

goal token hidden

Exploration

  • Hide that photo from the network.
  • The same weights wander sensibly instead — down corridors, around obstacles.

The five deep-dives

  1. P1 What goes in — the five pictures exactly as the model receives them.
  2. P2 Reading a picture — what each reader makes of one, drawn as a heatmap.
  3. P3 Weighing them up — attention with the goal token kept and with it hidden, side by side.
  4. P4 Noise into a path — all ten cleanup passes, one panel each.
  5. P5 The answer — the finished route three ways, and why asking again gives a different valid one.
